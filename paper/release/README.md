## Zenodo description
We study the pro-2 group P = (1+φO)/(1+tF₂[[t]]), O = F₈[[φ;σ]], φ³ = t, attached to the Cartwright–Mantero–Steger–Zappa
Ã₂-lattice of order 2, and the cokernel density c_e of the Hecke element F along its canonical tower
(ANCCFT Vol. III, §6.8–6.9). P is the first principal congruence subgroup of SL₁ of the degree-3 cyclic division
algebra over F₂((t)), and c_e = (1 − rk_P(F))/3, so c_e = 0 is a case of the pro-2 Atiyah conjecture not covered by
known results. Results:
(A) the restricted enveloping algebra of gr P is not a domain (explicit v with v² = 0 in degree 4), answering
Remark 6.9.2 of Vol. III negatively;
(B) gr P ⊗ F₈ is the positive part of the affine Lie algebra A₂⁽¹⁾ in its principal grading, and the leading form
of F is the cyclic sum of the Coxeter word, homogeneous of weight equal to the null root δ;
(C) explicit kernel elements at every level, with threshold d(m) ≈ m²/3 governed by a binary odometer along the
imaginary roots 3·2^r, and monotonicity of the kernel density;
(D) by exact computation up to |G₁₀| = 2²⁴, c_e ≤ 0.030322 (previously 0.0645);
(E) F is a non-zero-divisor in F₂[[P]]: its leading form is regular on u(n̂), proved with evaluation representations
of the loop algebra; hence k_m ≥ m − 3;
(F) c_e = 0 follows from two explicit hypotheses on the kernel of the leading form, verified for m ≤ 10 and m ≤ 14
(k₁₂ = 57, k₁₃ = 69, k₁₄ = 82, all computed exactly);
(G) a no-go theorem: every level-by-level argument bounds the threshold by O(m^{3/2}), exactly what the conditional
theorem cannot use, so a proof must be degree-sensitive (a Tor₁ vanishing theorem);
(H) exhaustive computations show that the graded zero divisors do not lift to F₂[[P]], consistent with the pro-2
Kaplansky/Atiyah conjecture.

Keywords: pro-p groups, Iwasawa algebras, completed group algebras, restricted Lie algebras, affine Kac–Moody algebras,
Ã₂ buildings, Hecke operators, Lück approximation, Atiyah conjecture, Kaplansky conjecture, sandpile groups,
cokernel density, computational algebra.
